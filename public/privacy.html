3. Public posts the lead finder processes

To find leads, APTO Pro sends search queries (a trade and a location — not your personal details) to a search API provider (SerpAPI), which returns publicly available web pages such as forum and community posts. Those results, which can include the names or usernames of the people who posted them, are analysed by our AI provider to decide whether they are genuine service requests. We show you a short summary and a link to the original public post.

We process this public information on the basis of our legitimate interests (and those of our users) in connecting people who have publicly asked for a service with local businesses that can help. We only use what is needed to show the lead, and we don't build profiles of the people who posted. If you have posted something publicly and want it excluded from APTO Pro, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

Users are responsible for contacting people lawfully and respectfully — see our Terms of Service.

4. Who we share data with

We use the following service providers (“processors”), who handle data only on our instructions:

  • Netlify, Inc. (USA) — website hosting, serverless functions and form submissions (directory enquiries and contact messages).
  • Supabase, Inc. — user accounts, authentication and our database. Data region: [SUPABASE DATA REGION, e.g. London (eu-west-2)].
  • Stripe Payments Europe Ltd / Stripe, Inc. — payments, subscriptions and the billing portal. Stripe is also an independent controller for some data (for example fraud prevention); see Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Anthropic PBC (USA) — AI analysis of leads and posts you paste in, and drafting suggested replies.
  • SerpAPI, LLC (USA) — web search results for the lead finder (receives trade and location search terms only).
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation (UK) — when you open the Lead Map in your dashboard, your browser loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap and looks up the location of your saved leads using its Nominatim service. This shares your IP address and those place names with OpenStreetMap.
  • jsDelivr — the dashboard loads a code library (Supabase's JavaScript client) from the jsDelivr content delivery network, which receives your IP address when it is downloaded.

Directory enquiries are shared with the business you contacted. That business then becomes responsible for how it uses your details.

Fonts: our fonts are hosted on our own website, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts when you visit.

We may also disclose data if required by law, or to protect our rights, users or the public.

5. International transfers

Some of our providers are based in, or access data from, the USA. Where personal data is transferred outside the UK or EEA we rely on adequacy regulations (including the UK–US “data bridge” for certified companies), the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and/or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, plus our providers' security measures.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, settings and lead data: while your account is open, and deleted within 90 days after you ask us to close it.
  • Payment and invoice records: 6 years after the end of the financial year they relate to, to meet UK tax law.
  • Directory enquiries and contact messages: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Hosting logs: kept by Netlify for a limited period in line with its own retention policy.
  • Data in your browser (saved leads, stats, last scan results) stays on your device until you clear it — see section 9.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

Under UK GDPR (and EU GDPR where it applies) you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Rectify data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • Erase your data (“right to be forgotten”) in certain circumstances;
  • Restrict how we use your data;
  • Data portability — receive data you gave us in a machine-readable format;
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests (including the processing of public posts described in section 3);
  • Withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent.

To exercise any right, email [CONTACT EMAIL] or use our contact form (choose “Privacy or data request”). We will respond within one month. It's free in most cases.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113. If you are in the EU, you can contact your local data protection authority.

8. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider, card details are handled only by Stripe, and access to our database and API keys is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

9. Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. The dashboard stores a sign-in token and your saved leads in your browser's local storage so it works. Full details are in our Cookie Policy.

10. Children

APTO Pro is a business tool for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

We'll update this page if our practices change, and tell account holders about significant changes by email or in the dashboard. Questions? Email [CONTACT EMAIL].
