Cookie Policy

No advertising or analytics cookies — just what is strictly needed to sign you in.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Short version: APTO Pro does not use advertising or analytics cookies. We only store what's strictly necessary to sign you in and make the dashboard work, so we don't need to ask for cookie consent.

What we store on your device

Instead of cookies, the APTO Pro dashboard uses your browser's local storage and session storage. These are covered by the same UK rules (PECR) as cookies, and everything below is strictly necessary for features you ask for.

NameTypePurposeHow long
sb-…-auth-tokenLocal storage (set by Supabase)Keeps you signed in to your dashboard.Until you sign out
apto_saved_leads, apto_stat_*, apto_day_*, apto_tradesLocal storageYour saved leads and your “My Results” statistics. These stay on your device and are not sent to us.Until you clear them
apto_pending_planLocal storageRemembers which plan you picked on the pricing page while you sign up.Until checkout or you clear it
apto_last_resultsSession storageLets you go back to your last scan results.Until you close the tab

The public pages of our website (home, Find a Trade, and these policy pages) don't set any cookies or local storage.

Third-party services

  • Stripe — when you go to checkout or the billing portal you are on Stripe's website, which uses its own cookies for security and fraud prevention. See Stripe's cookie policy.
  • OpenStreetMap — the Lead Map in your dashboard is embedded from openstreetmap.org, which may set its own cookies. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook pixels or any other tracking. Our fonts are hosted on our own site, so no request is made to Google Fonts.

Managing storage

You can clear local storage and cookies at any time in your browser settings (usually under Privacy → Clear browsing data / Site data). Signing out removes your sign-in token. If you clear everything, your saved leads and statistics stored in the browser will be deleted.

Changes and contact

If we ever add non-essential cookies (for example analytics), we'll update this page and ask for your consent first. Questions? Email [CONTACT EMAIL]. See also our Privacy Policy.
