About APTO Pro

An AI lead finder built for UK tradespeople and small local businesses.

What APTO Pro does

APTO Pro is an online lead-finding tool for UK tradespeople and small local businesses — plumbers, electricians, gardeners, decorators, cleaners, removal firms and many more.

Every day, people post on public forums, community sites and local groups asking things like “Can anyone recommend a plumber in Brighton?”. Those requests are easy to miss when you're busy on a job. APTO Pro searches public websites for requests that match your trade and area, uses AI to check whether each one is a genuine job request, scores how promising it looks, and writes a suggested reply you can edit and send yourself.

How it works

  1. Tell APTO your trade and area. It takes a couple of minutes to set up.
  2. APTO searches public sources. It looks at publicly available posts on forums, community sites and directories. You can also paste in a post you've spotted yourself (for example from a Facebook group) to have it analysed.
  3. You decide what to do. Each lead links back to the original post. APTO never contacts anyone on your behalf — you choose whether and how to reply.

Find a Trade directory

Our Find a Trade directory lets homeowners find local businesses that use APTO Pro and send them an enquiry. We pass enquiries on to the business; we don't charge commission and we don't take a cut of any job.

Simple pricing

APTO Pro is a flat monthly subscription with no per-lead fees. See the pricing section for the current plans. Payments are handled securely by Stripe and you can cancel at any time.
